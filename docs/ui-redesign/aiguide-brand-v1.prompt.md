## 图标完整生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Primary request: design one original brand icon for “AIGuide”, a Chinese AI tutorial and learning website.
This is the site's OWN identity, unrelated to the company logos displayed on its pages. Its meaning is finding a direction and learning how to use AI tools.
Create a single, exceptionally clean, vector-like app/favicon symbol. One icon only, centered, square composition, no wordmark and no written letters outside the icon.
Design concept: a confident geometric capital A integrated with a subtle upward guidance arrow. Use a dark near-black navy rounded-square tile (#111827) and a white solid monogram. The symbol's two broad diagonal legs form a clearly readable A; the upward-pointing apex conveys a compass/direction pointer, and a simple angled inner cutout/short crossbar gives a sense of a path moving forward. The A and directional cue must be ONE coherent shape, not separate symbols pasted together. Keep the negative space generous and optically balanced. Favor thick simple geometry over decorative strokes.
Make the monogram distinctive through the directional negative space, elegant and approachable. It must remain recognizable when reduced to 16px or 24px. Approximately 22% safe padding inside the tile; balanced corner radius around 22% of tile width.
Color: only navy #111827 and solid white. Flat, crisp, mathematically clean silhouettes. No gradients, no 3D, no bevels, no glossy material, no cast shadow, no texture, no extra colors.
The area OUTSIDE the rounded-square tile should be genuinely transparent alpha, not a checkerboard pattern, not an opaque white frame. The rounded-square tile nearly fills the canvas with a small even transparent margin. Deliver a high-resolution 1:1 square PNG-like icon.
Avoid an open-book icon, robot face, brain, generic sparkle/star, a stock compass illustration, an intricate AG monogram, or a copy of Appica's logo. No mockup, no device frame, no logo presentation board, no multiple options, no watermark. Render only the final original icon.
