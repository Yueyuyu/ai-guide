Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one realistic, polished desktop homepage for AIGuide, a comprehensive AI LEARNING WEBSITE in Simplified Chinese. It teaches how to use AI applications and understand AI models. Target dimensions 1600 x 1000, natural desktop website viewport, no browser or device frame, no board around the UI. Normal compact product scale: navigation 14px, body 16px, buttons 44px, headings maximum 46px. Plenty of breathing room, but not huge type or a vertically stretched composition. One clear primary action.
Critical feedback from the user: the previous designs did NOT look like the AI learning website they wanted. Those looked like an email-writing service, printed workbook, and poster creator. This design must be immediately identifiable as an AI tutorial and learning platform through actual Chinese AI curriculum and tool terminology. Users should see WHAT THEY CAN LEARN and WHERE TO BEGIN. Do not show a mail draft, coffee poster, notebook, massive slogan, giant generic art object, or a creative-service product. Do not use the previous mountains, seedling or paper-plane motifs. Avoid fake stats, paid membership, fabricated lesson counts, testimonial badges, certificate promises and dates.
Header uses the exact brand AIGuide and a small original precise professional brand mark, not a sparkle or robot. All other interface text must be Simplified Chinese except actual company and product names. Existing product names such as ChatGPT, Codex, Claude and Gemini remain in their original spellings. Distinguish tools from models; do not label ChatGPT as a model or GPT as an application. If software platforms are shown use Chinese "网页" and "桌面".
Create clear hierarchy, strong typography, purposeful spacing and intentional meaningful imagery. Show a focused primary screen, not a feature inventory. Only essential navigation and one or two supporting areas. Use whitespace and alignment first, simple dividers next, very subtle surface tints only if needed. Do not place the entire app inside a floating card or put cards inside cards. Limit shadows and avoid generic bento marketing layouts. No more than two fonts. Readable body type and natural proportions, nothing cropped at the edges.
