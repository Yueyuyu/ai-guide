```text

Match the reference artwork faithfully. No perspective, no device frame, no photoreal desk, no decorative background. All words inside the illustration are Simplified Chinese, except familiar filenames if specified. Render legible text.
Canvas 1024x448, horizontal wide 16:7 composition. Art should occupy about 88% width and 88% height, leaving even modest padding. Match the solid pastel background specified. Do not render an entire website. One image only. No logos, product name, banner headline, footer, watermark or surrounding border.
ASSET: the bottom LEFT tutorial from the reference. Solid background #eef5ff. A white document panel on the left with a small blue document symbol, the heading "材料" and six subtle horizontal gray text lines. A simple black arrow in the center points RIGHT to a second white rounded panel titled "要点", with three blue check circles next to "要点一", "要点二", "要点三" and a small bottom line "下一步". Make it feel like taking an uploaded document and turning it into an actionable summary. Each Chinese word must be accurate. Two panels side by side, about equal height. No computer window.
```
